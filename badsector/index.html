Minesweeper rebuilt as a hacking game. Every board can be solved by logic alone, three worlds change everything from the room to the cat, and every game ends with an analysis of where your time went.

Play a sector.

This is the game’s own engine, running in your browser. Before your first scan it builds a board, then a solver tries to clear it with logic alone. If it can’t, the board is thrown away. Whatever reaches you never needs a guess.

Worlds

Three worlds.
One board.

A world in BADSECTOR is more than a colour swap. It changes the room, the intro, the typeface, the words on screen, the cat’s voice, what lies under the hub and the story you uncover.

> safe, because

Stuck? Ask the cat.

NULL lights up a safe cell and the numbers that prove it, then says the rule out loud. You learn the pattern, not just the move. A game played with a hint still counts for you, but it stays out of records and rankings.

SINGLE

A number already touches all its ICE: every other neighbour is clean. Or it has exactly as many covered neighbours as ICE left: all of them are ICE.

PAIR

Two numbers share cells and one decides for the other: 1-1, 1-2, 1-2-1, 1-2-2-1.

COUNT

ICE left against covered cells: in the endgame the counter decides.

> after the game

Where did the time go?

Every game ends with an analysis. Pauses over four seconds are listed with what you missed: a safe move that was already waiting. Then press REPLAY and watch the whole game again, move by move.

> academy & campaign

Learn it. Then go in.

Tell NULL how much you know and it picks the lessons: twelve of them on three levels, each with puzzles built around one pattern. When you are ready, the campaign waits on the network map: twelve sectors that grow harder, each one with a briefing, and pieces of the story hidden along the way.

> codes

Sector codes

Every board has a code. Send it, and a friend plays the exact same sector from the exact same first cell.

> cat

NULL and RONIN

The cat talks in the hub, comments on your game, falls asleep when you leave it alone and has 25 things to say when you pet it. Or build your own in the creator.

> editor

Fork a world

Change colours, board style, glass and effects, then share your version as a single BS4 code.

> archive

Lore archive

Eight fragments per world, dropped by rare glitches, campaign sectors and the bottom of the layers.

> comfort

Your controls

Swap tap and hold, set the hold time, turn chording, vibration and glitch up or down, reduce motion, pick effect quality.

> offline

No signal needed

The whole game plays offline. Online rankings are opt-in and ask for nothing but a nickname.

Under the hub

Don’t dig
too deep.

Swipe down past the hub and the game keeps going: a terminal log, a district gone dead, something that watches. Go one layer too far and the system crashes. Every world has its own bottom: in Telemetry it is sector zero and a collapsing CRT, in Holo the projection folds into a single point.

Privacy

Offline by default.

The game needs no account and no connection: progress, statistics and settings live on your phone. Online rankings and usage statistics only start if you switch them on, and the privacy policy lists every field they send.
