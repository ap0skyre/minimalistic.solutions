Board codes, theme codes from the editor and result cards are made to be shared. Post them anywhere, with anyone. A theme you build from the game's parts is yours to share, but the parts themselves (the worlds, rooms, cats, fonts and art) stay ours and can only be used inside BADSECTOR.

Fair play

DAILY, WEEKLY, LEAGUE, ranks and the online rankings only mean something if everyone plays the same game. In them, do not use modified versions of the game, bots, solvers, scripted or emulated input, or anything else that plays for you.

Results are checked against the board's seed and the move log. Results that could not have been played fairly may be removed, and a player who cheats on purpose may lose access to the online rankings. The game itself, offline, stays yours.

A nickname in the online rankings must not impersonate someone, insult or harass anyone, or advertise anything. We may change or remove a nickname that does.

Online rankings and usage statistics are optional and off until you switch them on. They run on Google Firebase, as the privacy policy describes.
