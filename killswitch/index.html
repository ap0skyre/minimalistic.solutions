Killswitch
In development

Kill the feed.Keep theconversation.

Killswitch interrupts infinite scrolling in the apps you choose, while comments, chats and replies keep working.

Try the demo↓ Privacy policy↗

The feed ends.

  1. >

    Pick targets

    Tick the apps to guard on the TARGETS screen. The service listens to those packages and nothing else.

  2. >>

    Scroll

    Feed scrolls are counted against a time window. Comment threads, chats and focused text fields are recognised and left alone.

  3. >>>

    Get cut off

    Cross the threshold and a block screen shows for about three seconds, then Back is pressed for you.

  4. ///

    See progress

    Set your own hours-per-day BASELINE. The STATS screen tracks daily blocks and scrolls for the last 30 days.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone.

No account, no server, no analytics SDK, no tracking of any kind. Everything the app knows lives in its own storage on your phone, and uninstalling it deletes all of it.

Targets, counters, settings

Two permissions, fully explained.

AccessibilityService

Detecting the scroll

  • Receives scroll and app-switch events, only from the apps you ticked
  • Reads screen structure (view IDs, class names) to tell a feed from a thread
  • Never reads your text, messages or comments
  • No keylogging, no screenshots, no recording
  • Off until you switch it on, off whenever you want
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

Drawing the block screen

  • Shows the app name and your count for the day
  • Dismisses itself after about three seconds
  • No advertising, no tap collection
  • Never appears over apps you did not guard
