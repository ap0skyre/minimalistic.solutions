How it works

Your body is a character sheet.

  1. >

    Measure

    Enter weight, height, circumferences and body fat. Outlier calculates your real percentile from NHANES population data and a Gaussian model.

  2. >>

    Train

    Log workouts in Quick or Live mode. Pick one of 10 presets or build your own, with 89 animated guides keeping your form sharp.

  3. >>>

    Level up

    Climb from rank E to S. Your level tracks where you actually stand, so it moves when your body does.

  4. ///

    Dominate

    Track progressive overload, earn achievements and keep the streak alive. The cat starves if you skip training. Don't let the cat starve.

Everything in the kit

> live mode

Live workout

Per-set logging with rest timer, pause and resume, undo, extended cooldown and sound feedback per theme. Keeps running while you move around the app.

> percentile

Real rankings

See where you actually stand, not where an algorithm wants you to feel good.

> overload

Progressive overload

Automatic PR detection across your whole session history, with badges that change with the theme.

> animated

89 exercises

Smooth animation guide for every movement. Search by category, add mid-workout, merge templates on the fly.

> widget

Home screen widget

Level, top percentile and streak at a glance. Refreshes after every finished workout.

> streak

Streaks and a cat coach

Daily streak, quests, achievements, 10 easter eggs and a cat that gets hungry when you skip training.

> stats

Body composition

Navy body fat formula, percentile chart, goal line and history. Estimates are flagged with ≈ so you always know what is measured.

> local

Privacy first

Your data stays on the device by default. Google sign-in for cloud backup is optional. No ads, no data sales.
